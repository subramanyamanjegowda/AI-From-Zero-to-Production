# What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer or software system to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

These tasks include:

- Learning from data
- Understanding patterns
- Making decisions
- Solving problems
- Predicting outcomes
- Providing intelligent recommendations

In simple words, AI enables computers to perform tasks that traditionally required human thinking.

---

# Real-World Examples

Artificial Intelligence is already part of our daily lives.

### YouTube

YouTube recommends videos based on your watch history, search history, and interests.

### Instagram

Instagram studies the posts and reels you interact with and recommends similar content.

### Swiggy & Zomato

Food delivery applications recommend restaurants and dishes based on your previous orders and preferences.

### Netflix

Netflix suggests movies and TV shows based on your viewing history.

    A[User Data] --> B[AI Model]

Instead of following only fixed rules, AI learns from data and improves its decisions over time.

# Key Points

- Artificial Intelligence enables machines to perform intelligent tasks.
- AI learns from data instead of relying only on manually written rules.
- AI is widely used in recommendation systems, search engines, finance, healthcare, transportation, and many other industries.
